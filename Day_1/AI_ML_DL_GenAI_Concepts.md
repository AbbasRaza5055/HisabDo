# AI, ML, DL & Generative AI — Day 1 Learning Notes

This document covers the foundational concepts required for Day 1 of the internship.

---

## 1. What is AI (Artificial Intelligence)?

Artificial Intelligence is the broad field of computer science focused on building systems
that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence — such as reasoning,
decision-making, understanding language, and recognizing patterns.

AI is the **umbrella term**. Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI are all
subsets that fall under it.

**Real-world example:**
A spam filter in Gmail that decides whether an incoming email is spam or not is a basic
form of AI — it makes a decision that would otherwise require a human to read and judge
the email.

---

## 2. What is Machine Learning (ML)?

Machine Learning is a subset of AI where a system **learns patterns from data** instead of
being explicitly programmed with fixed rules. Instead of a developer writing "if X then Y"
for every possible case, the ML model is trained on historical data and learns the
relationship between inputs and outputs on its own.

Common ML techniques include regression, decision trees, and classification algorithms.

**Real-world example:**
Netflix recommending movies/shows to you based on your watch history. The system learns
patterns from millions of users' viewing behavior to predict what you might like.

---

## 3. What is Deep Learning (DL)?

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses **artificial neural networks**
with many layers ("deep" networks) to learn from large amounts of data. It is especially
powerful for complex, unstructured data like images, audio, and text — tasks where
traditional ML algorithms often struggle.

Deep Learning is behind most of the recent breakthroughs in AI (image recognition, speech
recognition, self-driving car perception, etc.).

**Real-world example:**
Face unlock on smartphones. A deep neural network analyzes the layers of facial features
in an image to verify identity in real time.

---

## 4. What is Generative AI (GenAI)?

Generative AI is a category of Deep Learning models that can **create new content** —
text, images, audio, video, or code — rather than just analyzing or classifying existing
data. These models are trained on massive datasets and learn to generate outputs that
resemble what they were trained on.

**Real-world example:**
ChatGPT generating a written response to a question, or tools like DALL·E/Midjourney
generating a new image from a text description.

---

## Quick Summary Table

| Concept          | Relationship                          | Core Idea                                   | Example                          |
|------------------|----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| AI               | Broadest field                        | Machines performing intelligent tasks         | Spam filter                       |
| Machine Learning | Subset of AI                          | Learning patterns from data                   | Netflix recommendations           |
| Deep Learning    | Subset of Machine Learning            | Neural networks with many layers              | Face unlock                       |
| Generative AI    | Subset of Deep Learning               | Creating new content (text/image/audio)       | ChatGPT, DALL·E                   |

**In short:** AI ⊃ Machine Learning ⊃ Deep Learning ⊃ Generative AI
(Generative AI is a specialized application built using Deep Learning techniques.)
