# PDF Export

## How do I export a PDF statement?

Open a customer's profile, tap "Export" or "Share Statement," and HisabDo
will generate a PDF showing their transaction history and current
balance. You can save it to your device or share it directly - useful
for giving customers a copy of their udhar record.

## What does the PDF include?

The exported PDF includes the customer's name, the date range of
transactions, a full list of individual transactions (credit given and
payments received), and the final running balance.

## Can I customize the PDF?

Depending on your app version, you may be able to add your business name
and logo to exported PDFs from Settings > Business Profile.

## Can I export statements for multiple customers at once?

Bulk export may be available from the main customer list under options,
depending on your plan - check for a "Export All" or similar option.

## Sharing a PDF directly with a customer

After generating the PDF, use the share button to send it directly via
WhatsApp, email, or any other app installed on your device.
