# Analytics

## What analytics does HisabDo provide?

HisabDo's analytics dashboard gives you a plain overview of your
business: total receivables, total transactions this month, and trends
over time so you can see how your business is doing without manually
reviewing every ledger entry.

## Viewing spending/earning trends

The Analytics tab shows a chart of your transaction activity over time,
letting you spot whether receivables are growing, shrinking, or staying
flat month over month.

## Top customers by balance

Analytics highlights which customers currently owe you the most, useful
for prioritizing who to follow up with.

## Category breakdowns

If you categorize your transactions, Analytics can show a breakdown of
activity by category (for example, how much of your recorded activity is
inventory purchases versus customer sales).

## Exporting analytics

Analytics summaries can be exported alongside your regular PDF
statements for record-keeping or sharing with an accountant.
