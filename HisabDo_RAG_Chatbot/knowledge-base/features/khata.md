# Khata (Ledger)

## Overview

The Khata is the core of HisabDo - your digital version of the
traditional business ledger notebook. It tracks every customer's running
balance based on the credit you've given them and the payments they've
made.

## How the running balance works

Each time you record a "credit given" transaction, the customer's balance
increases by that amount. Each time you record a "payment received," it
decreases. The Khata always shows the current, up-to-date balance for
every customer without you having to calculate it manually.

## Viewing your overall Khata

Your dashboard shows a summary of total receivables (money owed to you)
across all customers. Tap any customer to see their individual
transaction history and current balance.

## Sorting and filtering

You can sort your customer list by balance (highest first), name, or most
recent activity, and filter to show only customers with an outstanding
balance.

## Clearing a balance

When a customer fully pays off what they owe, recording their final
payment brings their balance to zero automatically - no manual
adjustment needed.
