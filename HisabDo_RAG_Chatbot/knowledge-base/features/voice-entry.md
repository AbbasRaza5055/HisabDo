# Voice Entry

## How does voice entry work?

Tap the microphone icon on the transaction entry screen and speak the
transaction details - for example, the customer's name and the amount.
HisabDo converts your speech into text and pre-fills a transaction entry
for you to review before saving.

## Which languages does voice entry support?

Voice entry supports the same languages as the rest of the app: English,
Urdu, Roman Urdu, Hindi, and Arabic. Make sure your language setting
matches the language you plan to speak in for best accuracy.

## What if voice entry gets something wrong?

Voice-entered transactions are always shown to you for review before
saving, so you can correct the customer name, amount, or transaction type
if the voice recognition made a mistake.

## Does voice entry work offline?

Voice entry may require an internet connection depending on your device
and app version, since speech recognition often relies on cloud
processing. Manual entry always works offline.

## Can I use voice entry to ask questions, not just record transactions?

Currently, voice entry is used for recording transactions. Voice-based
question answering (for example, asking "how much does a customer owe
me?" out loud) is a feature being explored for a future update.
