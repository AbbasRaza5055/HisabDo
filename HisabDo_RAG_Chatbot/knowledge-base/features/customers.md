# Customer Management

## Adding a new customer

Tap the "+" button on your dashboard, select "Add Customer," enter their
name and contact details (phone number is optional but recommended), then
save. You can start recording transactions for them right away.

## Customer profiles

Each customer has a profile page showing their current balance, full
transaction history, and contact information. From here you can add a
new transaction, edit their details, or export a PDF statement.

## Searching for a customer

Use the search bar at the top of your customer list to quickly find a
customer by name.

## Grouping or tagging customers

Depending on your app version, you may be able to tag customers (for
example, "Wholesale," "Regular," "New") to organize your customer list
for larger businesses with many customers.

## Removing a customer

You can archive or delete a customer from their profile page under
options. Archiving keeps their history for your records while removing
them from your active list; deleting removes them permanently.
