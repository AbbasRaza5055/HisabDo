# Getting Started with HisabDo

This guide walks you through your first few minutes using HisabDo.

## Step 1: Create your account

Download HisabDo and sign up using your phone number or email. You'll be
asked for basic business details, but you can skip most of this and fill
it in later.

## Step 2: Set your language

Go to Settings and choose your preferred language from English, Urdu,
Roman Urdu, Hindi, or Arabic. You can change this anytime.

## Step 3: Add your first customer

Tap the "+" button, select "Add Customer," and enter their name. Contact
details are optional but useful for later.

## Step 4: Record your first transaction

Open the customer you just added, tap "Add Transaction," choose "credit
given" if you're giving them udhar, enter the amount, and save. Try the
voice entry microphone icon too - it's often faster than typing.

## Step 5: Check your dashboard

Your dashboard now shows your total receivables and this customer's
balance. As you add more customers and transactions, this becomes your
full digital khata.

## Step 6: Enable cloud sync

Go to Settings > Backup & Restore and make sure cloud sync is turned on,
so your data is safely backed up and available if you switch devices.

## What's next?

Explore exporting a PDF statement, trying voice entry in your preferred
language, and checking the Analytics tab as your ledger grows.
