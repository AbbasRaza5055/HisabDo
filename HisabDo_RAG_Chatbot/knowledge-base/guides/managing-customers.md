# Managing Customers in HisabDo

A practical guide for keeping your customer list organized as your
business grows.

## Adding customers efficiently

If you have many existing customers to add, work through them one at a
time using "Add Customer," or check if bulk import is available under
Settings > Data Import for your plan.

## Keeping balances accurate

The most common mistake is forgetting to record a payment when it
happens. Try to log transactions as they occur rather than relying on
memory at the end of the day - voice entry can help make this quick even
when you're busy with customers.

## Following up on outstanding balances

Sort your customer list by balance (highest first) periodically to see
who owes the most, and use the Analytics tab to spot customers who
haven't paid in a while.

## Archiving inactive customers

If a customer hasn't had activity in a long time and has a zero balance,
consider archiving them to keep your active list focused on customers you
deal with regularly. Archived customers' history is preserved.

## Sharing statements

When a customer asks "how much do I owe?", export a PDF statement from
their profile rather than reading numbers off your screen - it's clearer
and gives them something to keep for their own records.
