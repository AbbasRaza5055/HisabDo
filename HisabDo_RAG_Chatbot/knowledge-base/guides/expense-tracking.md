# Expense Tracking in HisabDo

While HisabDo's core strength is tracking customer udhar, many users also
use it to track their own business expenses.

## Recording an expense

Expenses (like inventory purchases, rent, or utility bills) can be
recorded similarly to customer transactions - check whether your app
version has a dedicated "Expenses" section separate from customer khata,
or track them as notes within your transaction history.

## Categorizing expenses

If categorization is available, tag each expense (for example,
"Inventory," "Rent," "Utility Bill") so your Analytics tab can show a
breakdown by category over time.

## Why track expenses alongside receivables?

Seeing both what customers owe you AND what you're spending gives a much
clearer picture of your actual business health than tracking receivables
alone - two shops with the same udhar total can be in very different
financial positions depending on their expenses.

## Reviewing expenses over time

Use the Analytics tab to compare expenses month over month and spot
unusual increases early, rather than only noticing them at the end of the
year.

## Exporting expense records

Like customer statements, expense records can typically be exported as
PDFs for your own bookkeeping or to share with an accountant.
