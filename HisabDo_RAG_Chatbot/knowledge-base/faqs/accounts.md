# Account FAQs

## How do I create a HisabDo account?

Download HisabDo from the Play Store, open the app, and follow the sign-up
steps using your phone number or email. You can start recording
transactions immediately, even before completing full account setup.

## I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and follow the steps to reset
it using your registered phone number or email.

## Can I change my registered phone number?

Yes, go to Settings > Account > Phone Number to update it. You may need
to verify the new number with an OTP code.

## How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Note that this permanently
removes your ledger data, so it's recommended to export your records as
a PDF first if you want to keep a copy.

## Can multiple people use the same HisabDo account?

HisabDo accounts are designed for a single business owner. For shared
access with staff, check whether your plan supports team/staff accounts
under Settings > Team.
