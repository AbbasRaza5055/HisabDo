# Backup & Restore FAQs

## How does backup work in HisabDo?

Once cloud sync is enabled, your data backs up automatically whenever
you're connected to the internet. You don't need to manually trigger a
backup for normal use.

## How do I manually back up my data?

Go to Settings > Backup & Restore > Backup Now. This is useful before
switching devices or if you want an extra safety checkpoint before a
large batch of changes.

## How do I restore my data on a new device?

Install HisabDo on the new device, log in with your existing account, and
your data will automatically restore from the cloud backup, provided sync
was enabled on your previous device.

## What happens to my data if I lose my phone?

As long as cloud sync was enabled before you lost the phone, your data is
safe and can be restored by logging into your account on a new device.
If sync was not enabled, locally-only data cannot be recovered.

## Can I export my data outside of HisabDo?

Yes, you can export individual customer statements as PDF files from
their profile page. A full data export option may also be available
under Settings > Data Export, depending on your plan.
