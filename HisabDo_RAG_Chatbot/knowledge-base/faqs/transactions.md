# Transaction FAQs

## How do I record a new transaction?

Select a customer from your list (or add a new one), tap "Add
Transaction," choose whether it's credit given (udhar) or a payment
received, enter the amount, and save.

## Can I edit a transaction after saving it?

Yes, open the transaction from the customer's history and tap "Edit."
Note that editing a past transaction will update the customer's running
balance accordingly.

## Can I delete a transaction?

Yes, open the transaction and tap "Delete." Deleted transactions cannot
be recovered, so make sure before confirming.

## How do I record a transaction using voice?

Tap the microphone icon on the transaction entry screen and speak the
details - for example, the customer's name and the amount. HisabDo will
convert your speech into a transaction entry for you to review and
confirm before saving.

## What's the difference between "credit given" and "payment received"?

"Credit given" (udhar) means you gave a customer goods or services they
haven't paid for yet - this increases their outstanding balance.
"Payment received" means the customer paid you, which decreases their
balance.

## Can I attach a note or photo to a transaction?

Yes, when adding or editing a transaction, you can add a text note. Photo
attachments (such as a receipt image) may be available depending on your
app version.
