# General FAQs

## What is HisabDo?

HisabDo is a digital khata (ledger) application built for shopkeepers,
freelancers, wholesalers, and home-based sellers. It replaces the
traditional paper udhar/khata notebook, letting you record customer
credit, track payments, and manage your day-to-day business transactions
from your phone or computer.

## Is HisabDo free to use?

HisabDo offers a free tier suitable for most small business owners. Some
advanced features may require a paid plan as the app grows - check the
Settings > Subscription page in the app for current pricing.

## Does HisabDo work without internet?

Yes. HisabDo is built offline-first, meaning you can record transactions,
add customers, and view your ledger even without an internet connection.
Once you're back online, your data automatically syncs to the cloud so
it's backed up and available across your devices.

## What languages does HisabDo support?

HisabDo currently supports English, Urdu, Roman Urdu, Hindi, and Arabic.
You can change your language from Settings at any time, and the app
interface will update immediately.

## Is my financial data secure?

Yes. Your data is encrypted both on your device and during sync to the
cloud. HisabDo does not share your business or customer data with third
parties.

## Can I use HisabDo on multiple devices?

Yes, once cloud sync is enabled, your ledger data is available across any
device where you're logged into your HisabDo account.

## How do I contact support?

You can reach HisabDo support through the "Help & Support" section in the
app, or by emailing the support address listed on the HisabDo website.
